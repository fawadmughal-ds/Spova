# Zerventa — Instrument Data Sources

**As of 15 October 2026**

This document lists every tradable instrument on the Zerventa platform and whether its prices come from a **live market data feed** or from our **simulated (demo) pricing engine**.

**Summary: 289 instruments total — 135 on live real-market feeds, 154 on simulated pricing.**

Live data providers in use:
- **Binance** — cryptocurrencies
- **OANDA** — forex, metals, energy, and major indices
- **Finnhub** — US shares

## Making more instruments live

The remaining instruments can be switched to live data with a paid market-data subscription:

- **Major global indices** (JP225, FRA40, AUS200, HK50, etc.) — available on a funded OANDA live account.
- **Commodities** (coffee, copper, wheat, etc.) — require a commodities data provider.
- **The remaining ~119 shares** — require a full-market shares data provider (the free tier is limited to the top US names).

Each is a configuration change once a data subscription is in place — no platform rework is required.
